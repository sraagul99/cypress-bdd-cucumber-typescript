## Result definitions

- **Pass:** observed behavior matched the expected result.
- **Fail:** observed behavior differed from the expected result.
- **Blocked:** a prerequisite or external condition prevented meaningful execution.
- **Not Run:** no execution has taken place.
- **Not Applicable:** the case is outside the chosen scope or configuration; record a reason.
- **Skipped (automation):** the runner intentionally did not execute a scenario, for example due to missing authorized credentials. Do not convert this into Pass.
